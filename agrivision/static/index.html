🌱 Validated Agronomic Guidance & Prevention

Foliar fungal pathogen characterized by concentric ring lesions ('target board' effect).

Immediate Cultural Actions:
  • Prune severely spotted lower leaves using clean sterilized shears.
  • Discontinue overhead sprinkler watering to prevent spore dispersal.
Long-Term Prevention & IPM:
  • Apply organic straw mulch under plants to block soil-borne spores.
  • Implement 3-year crop rotation with non-solanaceous species.
